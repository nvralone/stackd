# Clover — Sprite & Audio Asset Manifest

## Source of truth
`assets/clover/reference.webp` is the supplied official artwork. Do not redesign Clover.
`assets/clover/clover_base.png` (172×169) is that same artwork with only the background card, the "12" label and the yellow excitement marks removed by `tool/crop_clover.dart`. No pixels of Clover were changed.

## Production spec (for a pixel artist)
- Match the reference exactly: silhouette, hood shape, dark eye mask, ear shapes, striped tail, palette (cream/white body, dark plum mask & markings, grey-violet tail stripes), outline weight.
- Native pixel grid: the reference is ~21×21 "art pixels" scaled ~8×. Deliver at **8 px per art pixel, 176×176 canvas**, transparent background, Clover's feet on the same baseline row as `clover_base.png`, same horizontal center.
- PNG-32, no anti-aliasing, no new colors except for small accents explicitly listed below.
- Same pose and body as the base unless the mood needs a body change (noted).

## Audio
Clover's voice is generated at runtime — **no audio files are required**:
- **Reactions:** `synthBabble()` builds a short raspy, pitch-bending syllable stream (16-bit WAV) whose pitch follows the mood (`CloverMood.pitch`, 0.65 dead-inside → 1.6 panicking). Played with `audioplayers`.
- **Recaps:** the device's built-in text-to-speech (`flutter_tts`) with pitch raised per mood. Device voices are generic; they will not sound like a unique character.
- Optional future clips (not required): `assets/clover/sfx/{laugh,gasp,groan,cheer,hmm}.wav`, ≤0.6 s, 22 kHz mono, recorded or synthesized by you (free sources only).

Settings: volume, mute (replay still plays), silent mode (nothing plays), subtitles, reaction frequency, TTS on/off. Without audio support, subtitles still show (offline fallback).
